The privacy contract (plain words)

Managers see pipeline and deal stage. They do not get your recordings, transcripts, coaching content, or development file. That’s how the system is built — not a settings toggle.

Yours

  • Tapes and transcripts
  • Coaching and drills
  • Role-play and goals
  • How you get better over time

What the company sees

  • Where deals sit in the pipeline
  • Stage of the job
  • Deal facts and next actions on the account

Your coach works for you — not as a write-up machine after a long day.

Coaching that never grades you

No scores. No report cards. No weakness leaderboards. The coach names what happened (with receipts from the conversation), sets the next process goal, and helps you rehearse it. You’ll never open the app to a red F.

The rehearsal edge

Before you knock: brief + mental rehearsal audio built for this lead. Breath work. Visualization. Your performance statement. Same idea pro athletes use — for the next kitchen table.

Role-play the hard conversation

Practice the exact customer you’re stuck on. Blow the close in practice, not in the living room. Live voice coach between stops when you need to talk it out.

Goals in your own words

Set goals by voice conversation. Wish, outcome, obstacle, plan — you confirm it. Daily lead measures that match how you actually work, not a form that dies in a drawer.

Solo closers welcome

Independent or 1099? Same coach. Solo early access — name, email, trade. We onboard a few people at a time and we’ll talk to you like a human.
