About

Built by home-service pros who close deals every day

We sold windows and roofs for a living — then built the coach we never had. One that hears the whole appointment, remembers every deal, and coaches like a pro trainer, not a report card.

The mission

The people who do skilled, honest work in real houses deserve the same caliber of coaching that white-collar sales teams take for granted. Every appointment deserves a coach — not once a quarter when a manager can spare a ride-along.

Omaha roots

Blue Collar Close is built in Omaha, Nebraska. Real people, trades DNA. Not a Silicon Valley team discovering “the trades” last quarter.

Commitments (principles as product)

In this category, trust is the feature.

We will never sell manager wiretaps on coaching

The rep is the client of the coaching. Managers get pipeline and stage — never transcripts or coaching content.

We will never grade a rep

No scores. No report cards. No leaderboards. Name what happened, set the next move, rehearse it.

We will never show a manager a transcript

By architecture. Not a checkbox buried in settings. That’s why reps can actually use it.

Ready when you are

Early access. We onboard a few teams at a time. We’ll call you.
