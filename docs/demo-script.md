# Blue Collar Close — Demo Script (v2.1 — ~4:07 as produced)

**Cast:** NARRATOR (voiceover) · COACH (the app's voice — warm, direct, asks before it tells) · MIKE (HVAC comfort advisor, works out of his truck)

**Production note:** Where coaching appears on-screen as text (recap, post-mortem, follow-up draft), COACH is voiced over the UI — standard demo convention. In the drill segment, COACH's voice is the feature.

Asset references point at this repo's [`assets/img/`](../assets/img/).

---

## COLD OPEN — The drive home (0:00 – 0:23)

VISUAL: Golden hour, wide shot. Mike gets into the work truck and pulls out of the driveway. Cut to the cab: he stares ahead at the road.

> **NARRATOR:** Every rep knows this moment. The one that got away — and no idea why. She was nodding right up until she said "let me think about it."
>
> **NARRATOR:** Ball games get film review. Sales calls just vanish. Unless your performance training app heard the whole thing.

VISUAL: Phone lifts into frame — app icon (`assets/img/svg/bcc-icon.svg`) → title card: **Blue Collar Close. The private AI sales coach for home-service reps.**

---

## BEAT 1 — The debrief (0:23 – 0:56) — record + evidence-anchored recap

VISUAL: Quick flashback: phone face-down on a kitchen counter, recording. Cut to now: lead profile call card → Recap (`assets/img/app/screen-lead-light.png`, then live scroll). A transcript line highlights as COACH cites it.

> **COACH:** Before I show you anything — what do you think happened when she went quiet at the end?
>
> **MIKE:** Honestly? I thought she was fine. She was nodding the whole time.
>
> **COACH:** Minute fourteen. She asked, "Is this the kind of thing people finance?" — and the conversation moved to install dates. That was a buying question. It never got an answer.

> **NARRATOR:** No grades. No lectures. Evidence — anchored to the words your customer actually said.

---

## BEAT 2 — The post-mortem (0:56 – 1:36) — when a deal dies, learn why

VISUAL: Mike drags the deal to Lost. Beat of silence. The post-mortem appears on the deal (live capture, dark UI `assets/img/app/screen-lead-dark.png` for the mood shift).

> **MIKE:** She went with the other guy. Just tell me straight — what killed it?
>
> **COACH:** Not price — she never pushed back on price. The financing question sat unanswered for nine days while their quote came with a monthly number. Next deal like this: when money comes up sideways, answer it head-on, that day.

> **NARRATOR:** Lost deals stop being mysteries. Every one ends with an honest post-mortem — and one lesson you carry forward.

---

## BEAT 3 — The drill (1:36 – 2:21) — role-play the moment that beat you

VISUAL: Mike driving, earbud in, phone in a dash mount — talking to Coach hands-free. Center button hold — voice ring animates, live caption pill. Audibly a spoken exchange over the earbud.

> **NARRATOR:** This is the drill. Coach plays the customer — the same objection that killed the deal — and Mike practices the answer out loud, until it can't rattle him.

> **COACH (as a customer, skeptical):** "Look, your quote's eleven hundred over the other guy's. Why wouldn't I just go with them?"
>
> **MIKE:** "Fair — and before you decide on the number, can I show you what each system costs you by year three? Because the cheap install is only cheap once."
>
> **COACH (as coach):** Better. You answered the money question with money — no dodge. Again — this time she interrupts you halfway.

> **NARRATOR:** Ten minutes in the truck, drilling the moment that beat you yesterday — until it can't beat you twice.

---

## BEAT 4 — Walk in ready (2:21 – 2:54) — breathwork + personalized visualization

VISUAL: Next morning. Earbud in. Appointment screen (`assets/img/app/screen-appointment-light.png`) → session screen: calm breathing visual, slow rhythm.

> **COACH (low, steady, over gentle audio):** In through the nose… hold… and out. Ten o'clock is the Hendersons — second floor won't cool, two kids up there all summer. When the money question comes this time, you have the year-three answer. See yourself saying it. Breathe in…

> **NARRATOR:** A few minutes of breathwork, then a visualization built for this appointment — your deal, your customer, your next move. Not a podcast. Yours.

---

## BEAT 5 — Close the loop (2:54 – 3:32) — follow-up drafts

VISUAL: Post-appointment. Recap → follow-up draft on the lead; thumb taps Copy, paste into Messages, send. (`assets/img/app/screen-today-light.png` for the return-to-Today close of the loop.)

> **NARRATOR:** And the follow-up? Coach has already drafted it — built from what was actually said in the appointment.
>
> **COACH:** Drafted your follow-up: "Hi Sarah — great talking through the upstairs system today. You asked about monthly options — here's the breakdown I promised, and I'm holding Thursday's install slot until Friday." Want it shorter?
>
> **MIKE:** That's it. Send-ready.

> **NARRATOR:** The follow-up writes itself from what was actually said — promised numbers, open questions, next steps. Ready to send before you're out of the driveway.

---

## BEAT 6 — Private means private (3:32 – 3:49)

VISUAL: Clean type-on over dark UI (`assets/img/app/screen-coach-dark.png`): **"Your coaching is yours. Full stop."**

> **NARRATOR:** And the part reps ask about first: your recordings, your drills, your post-mortems — they belong to you. Managers see the pipeline, not your tape. That's built into the product, not a policy page.

## Production notes

- The redemption arc is the spine: lose → post-mortem → drill the exact weakness → walk in calm → win the follow-through.
- **Claims discipline** (per this repo's CLAUDE.md): zero invented stats, no pricing, no unshipped features. The post-mortem beat maps to the shipped Sale/Dead post-mortems; the drill beat to the voice role-play path; breathwork/visualization to the shipped session pipeline; the privacy beat to the enforced manager boundary (managers cannot access coaching content).
- On-screen text is never AI-generated: UI text comes from real app captures; title cards, captions, and the type-on are rendered from HTML/CSS using this site's design tokens (`assets/css/site.css`).
